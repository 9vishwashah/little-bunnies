
## SHIPPING POLICY

Little Bunnies strives to give its customers high quality products along with smooth & fast, safe & secure online ordering experience. To deliver you the best, we give utmost priority to our logistic management and some of the key features are mentioned below:

• FAST, SAFE & RELIABLE SHIPPING PARTNER

We at Little Bunnies only trust the best and so we have officially partnered with world’s top logistic partners BLUEDART & DELIVERY, to make sure all your orders are delivered hassle free at your doorstep in shortest time.

• STRONG TAMPERPROOF PACKAGING

We use high-quality printed recyclable box packaging for most of our standard size shipments. For all other orders, secured tamper & waterproof courier bags or corrugated cartoons are used with complete Little Bunnies tapes securing the parcels from any transit damage or soiling.

• FASTEST SHIPMENT & DELIVERY TIME

Most of Little Bunnies products are Personalized and thus every piece is unique and it’s always a challenge to process orders in quick time. Therefore, we cover up delivery time and ship all orders through fastest shipping modes based on pin-code. Once shipped, most of the orders are delivered within 24-72 hrs across India . In case the shipment is not received even after 4 days of receiving the shipment alert, please contact our support team for the needful.

• SEAMLESS TRACKING & ORDER UPDATES

We extremely value your order and therefore throughout your order journey, our team along with highly automated system keeps you connected and updates you at every stage; i.e. order received, processing, completed, shipped with tracking details. All updates are sent via. WhatsApp, E-mails & SMS on registered phone number and email. Further, you can contact our team on working days in business hours (11.00 a.m. -7.00 p.m.) via WhatsApp on support phone numbers.

• DELIVERY TIME & LOCATIONS

Most of the products are delivered within standard delivery time (mentioned with each product). We deliver to all serviceable Pin Codes (as per delivery partners) across India. Deliveries to few places like Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep Islands, some North East States, and Jammu & Kashmir etc. may be at our discretion. For over-weight & high volume items, where there are additional charges our team may get in touch to do the needful. Currently we are only shipping within India. However, in case you need to ship outside India , please get in touch with us to arrange that separately at additional charges.

• FREE SHIPPING ACROSS INDIA

All of Little Bunnies orders [above Rs. 990/-] are shipped free across India . However, from time to time we may charge [peak season/ fuel surcharge amount] to cover up our logistics charges to provide you best of our services.

• REVERSE PICK-UP FACILITY

In cases where it is required, we also have reverse pick-up facility available wherein the customer needs to just hand over the order back in proper condition without packing to our shipping partner agent. Rest is taken care by us.


### SHIPPING RELATED FAQ’S

- Delivery time is the total number of working days for an order to be delivered to your doorsteps from the day it has been ordered.
- Processing Time is the total number of standard working days required for your ordered to be ready at our manufacturing facility before it is shipped.
- Shipping Time is the number of working days required for your order to reach you once it is shipped from our facility.
DELIVERY TIME = PROCESSING TIME + SHIPPING TIME

‣ What does ‘EXPRESS’ tag means on a product ?

Express tags (green color) on a product means that the product can be delivered within 5-7 days of ordering. However, in exceptional cases and during seasonal time like festivals, New Year, Christmas etc. , it may take little longer upto 15 days.

‣ What does ‘SPEED-UP’ means as mentioned in some products ?

**CURRENTLY UNAVAILABLE**

Little Bunnies follows a policy of FIFO in processing and shipping its orders. Certain products which are made to order customized products have long standard processing time. However, SPEED-UP is an option where the product processing can be prioritized by paying a little extra and thus reducing delivery time significantly.

‣ What is shipping method ‘Quick Ship Via Bluedart Air’?

**CURRENTLY UNAVAILABLE**

This is a default express mode of shipment for all orders received. No extra charges apply.

‣ What is the delivery timeline for orders with multiple products?

All the products in a single order are shipped together. Therefore it is important to remember that delivery timeline of the product with highest delivery time will apply to the entire order. In case you need to get some products delivered faster, please mention a note while ordering or get in touch with our customer care team and they will be more than happy to assist.

‣ How do I change my shipping address?

Shipment address can be changed by contacting our customer care team via WhatsApp or call before the order is shipped. Please note that in case the shipment has left our facility, changes cannot be made and all costs and charges of reshipment once received back will be borne by the customer.

‣ Can I split and ship an order to multiple locations?

Each order is shipped only to a single destination address. If you wish to ship products to different addresses, you are advised to create multiple orders with different shipping addresses. In rare cases, if an order needs to be split, please contact our customer team and they will do the needful.
